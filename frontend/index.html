How to play

Run one household day on a fixed energy budget

The goal
A day plays out live from 00:00 to 24:00. Switch appliances on and off by clicking them in the house or on the cards below it.
Finish under the energy budget while keeping the household comfortable.
The refrigerator must run all day and the cooktop for 2 hours. Missing either costs 200 points each.
Random events (heatwave, guests…) change what the household wants that day.
How to score well
The air conditioner and water heater use the most energy. Use them wisely.
Power is cheapest 22:00–06:00 and most expensive 17:00–22:00. Move laundry, water heating and ironing to the night.
Watch the ring and the "on pace" line to stay under budget.
Keys: Space pauses, 1–0 toggle appliances. Use 2× or 4× to speed the day up.
